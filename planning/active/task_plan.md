# Task: Vignette maps are correct but do not communicate; salmon habitat is missing from the legend (#61)

## Context

Re-rendered and read both vignette figures on 2026-08-26. They are *correct* —
every colour traces to `gq_reg_main()`, which is what they were built to prove —
and they are hard to read. Proving that every layer is styled from the registry
is not the same as making a map that says what it is about.

The headline defect: the most visually prominent thing on the composition map is
a bright red 397-feature salmon-habitat network, and **it is not in the legend
at all**. The prose directly beneath the figure describes that layer's three
registry widths and its intermittent dash (#36) — a feature the reader cannot
locate.

## What exploration changed about the scope

**The labels are wrong at source, and this is a three-month-old known bug with
three duplicate trackers and three consumer workarounds.**

`ACCESS` is translated through the *barrier-status* vocabulary, so
`ACCESS;ASSESSED` reads `"No known barriers; known barrier"`. Confirmed present
in the QML corpus itself (`inst/styles/vector/streams_{salmon,st,bt}.qml`, 10
classes each) — gq extracted it faithfully. Already tracked in:

| issue | what it says |
|---|---|
| bcfishpass#13 | source `.qlr`; token1 `ACCESS` should read **"Accessible"** |
| gq#33 | tracking-only: "regenerate after upstream lands" |
| gq#37 | duplicate of #33; proposes relabelling in the registry |

And already worked around, independently, in **three** consumer repos —
`link`'s PARS vignette, `fish_passage_peace_2025_reporting/0760-*.Rmd:158-171`,
and `fish_passage_template_reporting#219` — each carrying its own hand-rolled
token decoder. That is the real cost: the registry exists so consumers don't do
this, and three of them are doing it.

So Phase 1 is not vignette polish. It deletes three copies of a workaround and
closes two gq issues.

**The override cannot be a hand-edit.** `inst/registry/reg_main.json` is
generated by `data-raw/reg_build_main.R`, so an edit is silently reverted on the
next rebuild. And `gq_reg_merge()` replaces at **whole-layer** granularity
(`R/gq_reg.R:197,202`), so routing it through `reg_custom.csv` would mean
re-authoring 90 classes by hand across three layers. It has to be a targeted,
guarded correction step inside the build script.

**Two things the Explore agent corrected me on**, recorded so they aren't
re-derived: `gq_tmap_legend()`'s collapse keys on the *whole row including the
label* (`R/gq_tmap_legend.R:211-225`), so the 5-colour repetition does **not**
merge — 12 present classes give 12 entries; and a caller-supplied name
(`c("Salmon habitat" = "streams_salmon")`) is silently discarded for classified
layers (`:147` is in the unclassified branch only).

## Decisions taken

- **Override in gq**, don't wait for upstream (user).
- **Drop the `;INTERMITTENT` rows from the legend** and explain the dash once in
  prose — 12 line entries rather than 18 (user).
- Correct label text is **"Accessible"**, per bcfishpass#13 — not "Migration",
  which is what I had guessed before reading it.
